About

I am a 4th-year applied mathematics Ph.D. student at the University of Colorado Boulder (CU Boulder), where I am advised by Adrianna Gillman. My research interests are broadly in numerical partial differential equations and applications of functional, numerical, and asymptotic analysis techniques. In particular, I enjoy working on boundary integral equations methods for elliptic PDE and designing associated “fast” algorithms. I received bachelor's (BSc) and master's (MS) degrees in applied mathematics from CU Boulder and am expected to graduate from the doctoral program in May of 2028. For more on my professional experience, please see my CV: PDF

Outside of teaching and research, I enjoy serving as the president of CU Boulder's Society for Industrial and Applied Mathematics (SIAM) Graduate Student Chapter, which hosts regular graduate student and alumni talks aimed at fostering interconnectedness in research amongst graduate students. I also enjoy trail running and rock climbing in and around Boulder, CO.
