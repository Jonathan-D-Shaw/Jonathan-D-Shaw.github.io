About

I am a 4th-year doctoral student at the University of Colorado Boulder (CU Boulder), where I am advised by Adrianna Gillman. My current research interests include computational methods for partial differential equations (scattering and inverse problems, in particular) and fast solvers for integral equations. I received my bachelor's degree (BSc) in applied mathematics with a concentration in aerospace engineering from CU Boulder and my master's of science (MS) from CU Boulder in May 2026 on my way to the Ph.D. For more on my professional experience, please see my CV: PDF

Outside of teaching and research, I enjoy serving as the president of CU Boulder's Society for Industrial and Applied Mathematics (SIAM) Graduate Student Chapter, which hosts regular graduate student and alumni talks aimed at fostering interconnectedness in research amongst graduate students. I also enjoy trail running and rock climbing in and around Boulder, CO.
