About

Hi! Thank you for visiting my personal web page. I am a 3rd-year doctoral student at the University of Colorado Boulder (CU Boulder), where I am advised by Eduardo Corona. My current research interests include computational methods for partial differential equations (particle Stokes flows, in particular) and fast solvers for large, highly-oscillatory, stiff systems in the context of power systems simulations. I received my bachelor's degree (BSc) in applied mathematics with a concentration in aerospace engineering from CU Boulder and am slated to receive a master's of science (MS) in May 2026 on my way to the Ph.D. For more on my professional experience, please see my CV: PDF

Outside of teaching and research, I enjoy serving as the president of CU Boulder's Society for Industrial and Applied Mathematics (SIAM) Graduate Student Chapter, which hosts regular graduate student and alumni talks aimed at fostering interconnectedness in research amongst graduate students. I also enjoy trail running, cycling, and rock climbing in and around Boulder, CO.
